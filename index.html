Image Resizing & Fitting Guide

🚀 How to Use ImageFit

1. Add Photos: Drag and drop files onto the box or click "Browse Files".

2. Choose Your Size: Pick a preset (e.g., Instagram 1080×1080) or type custom pixels.

3. Pick How to Fit: Select whether you want borders added, edges cropped, or natural ratio preserved.

4. Download: Click "Process All Images" then download individually or as a single ZIP archive.

📐 Fitting Modes Explained

• Fit inside (Contain): Centers the whole photo inside your target dimensions and fills empty borders with your chosen background color. Nothing is ever cropped or cut off. Ideal for product listings and catalog standardization.

• Fill entire frame (Cover): Zooms and neatly trims the excess edges so the entire box is filled with no borders. Great for social feeds, avatars, and website banners.

• Stretch to exact size: Ignores the photo's original proportions and forces width and height to match exactly.

• Scale by percentage: Multiplies width and height by a percentage (e.g., 50% makes it half the size).

• Limit longest edge: Keeps the original shape and shrinks the photo down so its longest side does not exceed your limit.

📱 Common Resolution Standards

• Square Post: 1080 × 1080 px (1:1)

• Story / Reel / TikTok: 1080 × 1920 px (9:16)

• YouTube Thumbnail: 1280 × 720 px (16:9)

• E-Commerce Product: 1000 × 1000 px

• Full HD Screen: 1920 × 1080 px
